Services

Each client is unique.

Are you or your organization looking to reach your greatest potential and progress in a strategic manner? This is where I can help.

My services are tailored to meet your needs.

Leadership coaching

Whether you are an individual, team or organization, leadership coaching helps clients gain perspective and excel in their professional career. We focus on the three areas of leadership development of mindset, style and skill. I help clients identify their strengths and growth areas to successfully navigate their career.

Career coaching

I support individuals in gaining clarity and progressing their career in a strategic, confident manner. This can also include supporting leaves such are parental leave, helping individuals with making the most of their Performance Improvement Plans or Outplacement Services.

Retirement coaching

Getting ready for the next phase? Retirement coaching can empower you, your organization and employees to plan for a successful and rewarding transition. I work with individuals and organizations to plan the next step with intention, focussing on interests and skill. Retirement coaching encompasses of review of two broad areas being schedule (how you will spend your time) and identity (what you will do) to help you move forward with clarity and confidence. This can also include succession planning.
