About Terri

I focus on providing a service that is tailored for each client based on their unique circumstance. My clients include individuals and organizations looking to reach their potential and progress in a strategic manner.

I have developed my expertise through:

Lawyer and mediator

I practiced law for 13 years before shifting to supporting the professional development of others. I continue to hold my active practicing licence as a lawyer and have mediation training and experience. Through my career I have gained experience in a variety of settings from small and large business to a large academic setting. This also includes being an entrepreneur.

Coach

As an ICF ACC certified coach with over 20 years of experience I provide services to individual clients, teams and organizations across Canada. This includes being on the coach roster for a number of institutions including top Canadian law firms.

My training and approach are solutions focused. I work with clients to tap into their strengths and utilize their potential to move forward in a positive manner.

Workshop facilitator

For over 20 years I have enjoyed facilitating a variety of workshops. This includes the CPLED PREP Bar Course, various organizations, and the CBA.
